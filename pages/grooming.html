01 / CAMPUS READY

Your college grooming ritual

College grooming should be realistic: quick enough for busy mornings, comfortable enough for long days and polished enough for presentations or meeting new people. The goal is not heavy makeup — it's looking clean, fresh and intentional.

01

Start with clean skin

Wash your face according to your skin's needs. If you have been sweating, cleanse after the activity. Keep the routine gentle rather than constantly adding new products.

02

Moisturize appropriately

Use a moisturizer that suits your skin type. The aim is comfortable, hydrated skin rather than a heavy layer of products.

03

Don't skip sun protection

If you are going outdoors during the day, use a broad-spectrum, water-resistant sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher. Apply before going outside and reapply when appropriate during extended outdoor exposure.

04

Keep hair intentional

Choose a hairstyle that stays comfortable throughout lectures. Avoid excessive heat styling. If your hair is wet, detangle gently rather than aggressively.

05

Freshness matters

Clean clothes, fresh breath, trimmed nails, deodorant if you use it, and a tidy bag can make your overall presentation feel much more put together.

06

Keep makeup optional

If you enjoy makeup, a simple combination such as lip balm, subtle blush and groomed brows can create a polished everyday look without requiring a long morning routine.

02 / OFFICE READY

Professional without overdoing it

Professional grooming is mainly about consistency and cleanliness. You don't need expensive products or a complicated routine. Focus on neat hair, comfortable skin, fresh hygiene and an outfit appropriate for the workplace.

01

Plan your outfit the night before

Check your clothes, shoes and accessories before sleeping. This removes morning stress and gives you time to notice wrinkles, stains or missing pieces.

02

Keep hair neat and practical

Choose a hairstyle that stays controlled during your workday. Avoid repeatedly touching or adjusting your hair during meetings.

03

Keep the face fresh

Use a simple skincare routine that your skin tolerates. Daytime sun protection is especially useful if your commute or work involves being outdoors.

04

Keep fragrance subtle

If you use fragrance, a light application is generally more workplace-friendly than a strong scent.

05

Hands and nails

Clean hands and neatly maintained nails make a practical difference in professional settings, especially when working with people or equipment.

06

Confidence is part of grooming

Sit and stand comfortably, maintain appropriate eye contact, speak clearly and avoid constantly checking your appearance.

03 / TRIP READY

Your travel grooming ritual

Travel can mean sun, sweat, dry air, unfamiliar water, long journeys and changes in your normal routine. Keep your kit small and focus on the essentials.

01

Build a small grooming pouch

Carry travel-sized versions of the products you already know work for you. Avoid experimenting with several new products just before a trip.

02

Sunscreen is a travel essential

Outdoor trips increase sun exposure. Use broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen and reapply approximately every two hours when outdoors, and after swimming or sweating.

03

Protect your hair

Sun and swimming can stress hair. A hat can provide physical protection, and gentle washing plus conditioner can help keep hair manageable.

04

Hygiene during the journey

Wash your hands with soap and clean water when possible, especially before eating and after using the toilet. Hand sanitizer can be useful when soap and water are unavailable.

05

Don't forget your basics

Pack toothbrush and toothpaste, deodorant, comb, hair ties, tissues, moisturizer, sunscreen, lip balm and any personal hygiene products you regularly use.

06

After the trip

Return to your normal routine. Clean reusable grooming tools, wash clothes appropriately and give your skin and hair a break from unnecessary styling.
